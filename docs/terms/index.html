The service

TFSA Tracker ("the app") helps Canadian residents track Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA) contribution room. Features include manual tracking of contributions and withdrawals, contribution-room calculations, and optional automatic import of account activity through Plaid.

Not tax or financial advice

The app is a tracking aid, not an official CRA statement. Contribution-room figures, excess warnings, and tax estimates are calculations based on the information you provide and the limits you enter. Your official TFSA room is determined by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA). We are not responsible for discrepancies, penalties, or tax consequences arising from reliance on the app's estimates. Verify your room with the CRA (My Account) before making contribution decisions.

Connected accounts

If you connect financial accounts through Plaid, you authorize us and Plaid to access and import the account and transaction information you select, on an ongoing basis until you disconnect. You can disconnect at any time in Settings; disconnecting stops further imports and deletes the imported data for that connection.

Automatic imports depend on your financial institution and Plaid. Imports may be delayed, incomplete, or misclassified (for example, transfers between two TFSAs may initially appear as a withdrawal and a contribution). Review your activity and delete or correct entries as needed.
